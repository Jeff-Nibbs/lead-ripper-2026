# Deep Research: AI Automation Businesses in Rocklin & Roseville, CA

**Research date:** July 3, 2026
**Scope:** AI automation, AI consulting, workflow automation, AI agents/chatbots, marketing automation, and RPA businesses located in or serving Rocklin, CA and Roseville, CA (South Placer County, Sacramento metro), plus the market context surrounding them.
**Method:** Three parallel research agents (Rocklin businesses, Roseville businesses, regional market context) ran ~33 web searches and ~16 targeted site scrapes via Firecrawl, with every claim cited to a source URL. *Note: Apify was requested for this research, but no Apify token/integration was available in the execution environment, so Firecrawl (the configured web-data provider) was used instead. To use Apify actors (e.g., the Google Maps scraper) in a future run, add an `APIFY_TOKEN` or the Apify MCP server to the environment.*

---

## Executive Summary

**Bottom line: roughly 15–17 genuine local players exist across the two cities, but almost none of them sell SMB-focused AI automation (voice agents, chatbots, workflow builds) — that exact niche is being harvested remotely by out-of-town agencies running programmatic local-SEO pages. The "Rocklin/Roseville AI automation agency" position is effectively unclaimed.**

Key findings:

1. **Roseville has the real supply (~8–10 verified local firms), concentrated in one corridor.** Five of the strongest — Symphonize, WindFarm Marketing, Three Ventures, BlastX Consulting, and Solvd — sit at 920/950/970 Reserve Dr off Douglas Blvd. Xen.AI (501 Gibson Dr) is the purest AI company in the city. These firms skew enterprise (healthcare, banking/financial services, enterprise CX) with offshore delivery arms.

2. **Rocklin supply is thin (~7 verified, only 2 truly local service firms).** HQ Software Consulting (AI/ML consulting since 1995) and Wordflirt (boutique AI marketing automation) are the only high-confidence locally headquartered service providers; the rest are AI product companies (TruckBook, HuLoop, Essential Aero) or branch offices (Sourcepass MSP).

3. **The SMB gap is being served remotely.** Agencies in Calgary (The Automators), Oregon (Lithium Marketing), Seattle (AutomateNexus), San Diego (AI Agents LLC), and Sacramento (ARMS Digital) all run dedicated Rocklin/Roseville landing pages selling AI receptionists, voice agents, and chatbot builds — proof of local demand with no dominant local supplier.

4. **Demand signals are concrete.** Rocklin-HQ'd Premier Pool Service is hiring an in-house AI Automation Engineer ($60k–$180k); Roseville's city-backed Venture Lab runs monthly "AI for Business" workshops; Adventist Health (Roseville HQ) restructured 750 back-office roles around efficiency; the Sacramento Valley SBDC runs free AI trainings that prime SMB demand.

5. **The addressable market is ~10,000 businesses** (Rocklin ~2,700–4,000; Roseville ~6,900) in two affluent, fast-growing cities. Best-fit verticals: healthcare-adjacent practices, home services, auto dealers (Roseville Automall, 21 brands), retail, and professional services. National SMB pricing norms ($500–$2,000/mo retainers; $3K–$10K setup projects) should transfer without discounting.

6. **Lead-gen channels are well-defined:** Rocklin Area Chamber (600+ members), Roseville Area Chamber (100+ owners per breakfast), BNI Capital Region chapters, SBDC AI trainings, and the Build Up Rocklin accelerator.

The report is organized in three parts: **Part 1 — Rocklin business directory**, **Part 2 — Roseville business directory**, **Part 3 — Market context & lead-generation implications**.

## Summary & Market Observations

**Verified genuine local players: ~8–10** (Xen.AI, Symphonize, Three Ventures, Solvd, BlastX, WindFarm, Holm Marketing, WebVello, plus lower-confidence OSKI and Bay Area Coders).

1. **The Reserve Drive cluster.** Five of the strongest local companies sit within one office corridor off Douglas Blvd: 920 Reserve Dr (Symphonize #100, WindFarm #180), 950 Reserve Dr (Three Ventures #100, BlastX #150), 970 Reserve Dr (Solvd #205). Anyone prospecting Roseville AI firms can practically walk the block.

2. **Bimodal market.** Local supply splits into (a) enterprise-grade AI/engineering firms with offshore delivery (Xen.AI, Symphonize, Solvd, BlastX) targeting healthcare/BFSI/enterprise CX, and (b) marketing-flavored automation (Three Ventures, WindFarm, WebVello, Holm).

3. **Gap: local SMB-focused AI automation.** There is no strong *Roseville-based* agency doing AI voice agents / receptionists / chatbot automation for local SMBs — that exact demand is being harvested by out-of-town programmatic-SEO players (The Automators from Calgary, Lithium from Oregon, AutomateNexus from Seattle), all of whom built dedicated Roseville pages. Holm Marketing (GHL automation) is the closest local entrant but is marketing-first and small.

4. **Demand signals.** City-backed Roseville Venture Lab runs free monthly "AI for Business" workshops for small businesses (https://www.roseville.ca.gov/articles/AI-for-Business-Practical-Tools-for-Small-Businesses-620.php); Adventist Health (Roseville HQ) hires marketing-automation staff; a Roseville business-process-automation provider was acquired by Naviant, an RPA (Kryon) partner (https://madisonbiz.com/naviant-inc-acquires-business-process-automation-provider-in-roseville-ca/). ## 2. Demand Signals for AI/Automation

- **Healthcare is the anchor vertical.** Sutter Roseville just added 36 beds to become Placer County's largest hospital; Kaiser is expanding Roseville facilities ([Facebook/local news](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1507129072837539/posts/4051968451686909/); [Sacramento Bee](https://www.sacbee.com/news/local/health-and-medicine/article213558949.html)). Adventist Health (HQ in Roseville) publicly uses AI for triage/diagnostics and restructured 750 back-office roles to "improve efficiency" — a signal that ops automation is on healthcare execs' agenda ([Adventist Health Policy](https://adventisthealthpolicy.org/ai-use-in-the-health-care-space); [Becker's](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/finance/adventist-health-restructures-back-office-services-750-workers-affected/)). The big systems buy enterprise; the realistic AI-agency market is the ecosystem around them (private practices, dental, PT, home health, medical billing).
- **City government is tech-forward.** Roseville brands itself a "smart city" with a published citywide technology work plan ([City IT dept](https://www.roseville.ca.gov/departments/information_technology/index.php); [IT Work Plan PDF](https://www.roseville.ca.gov/Documents/Departments/Information%20Technology/About/Strategic%20Planning/IT_WorkPlan_24_25_Digital.pdf)); an AI data center proposal was recently before the Planning Commission ([Modesto Bee via Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/modestobee/posts/1482434900594008/)).
- **Retail/auto volume creates SMB demand.** Westfield Galleria: 240+ stores, 2,500 employees, $730M annual sales ([CBS Sacramento](https://www.cbsnews.com/sacramento/news/roseville-galleria-celebrates-25-years/)); Roseville Automall hosts 21 dealership brands ([rosevilleautomall.com](https://www.rosevilleautomall.com/)) — dealerships are heavy adopters of AI voice/chat/lead-follow-up tools.
- **SMB AI education is actively happening**: Sacramento Valley SBDC runs free "Intro to AI for Small Businesses" trainings ([SBDC](https://www.sacramentovalleysbdc.org/event/intro-to-ai-what-small-businesses-need-to-know/)); free hands-on AI programs for businesses in Rancho Cordova ([StartupSac](https://startupsac.com/startup-events-week-of-september-1/)). Rocklin itself runs "Build Up Rocklin," a business acceleration program with Growth Factory, now in its third cohort ([City of Rocklin](https://www.rocklin.ca.us/node/5401)).
- **The gap is real:** a local audit-based report claims ~27% of Rocklin businesses have no website, ~90% of audited local businesses have zero organic presence, and local businesses miss ~62% of inbound calls (~$126K/yr lost) — exactly the problems AI receptionists/chatbots/workflow automation solve ([PipelineOS](https://localbusinesspipeline.com/blog/rocklin-small-business-statistics-2026/); note: vendor-published, directionally useful).

## 4. Pricing Norms (US SMB AI automation)

- Small-business retainers typically **$500–$2,000/month**; mid-market **$3,000–$8,000/month** ([Taskip](https://taskip.net/ai-automation-agency-pricing/) — packages span $500 to $20K+)
- Project builds: simple workflow automation **$3,000–$10,000**; more complex builds **$10,000–$35,000**; chatbots around **$500 setup + up to $500/month per bot** ([Arsum](https://arsum.com/blog/posts/ai-automation-agency-pricing/))
- AI automation consulting engagements for small business run **$5K–$75K** overall ([Layer3 Labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/ai-consulting-for-small-business))
- Hybrid model gaining traction: ~**$4K/month core "AI ops" retainer** plus per-workflow fees ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-agency-services-pricing-strategies-2026))
- No published Rocklin/Roseville-specific AI-automation price points were found; the closest local proxy is web/SEO pricing content from PipelineOS ([e.g., website cost in Rocklin](https://localbusinesspipeline.com/blog/how-much-does-website-cost-rocklin-ca)). Given local median household income >$105K and healthy service businesses, national SMB pricing should transfer without discounting.

## Implications for Lead Generation Targeting Rocklin/Roseville

1. **The TAM is ~10,000 businesses** (Rocklin ~2,700–4,000 + Roseville ~6,900) in two affluent, fast-growing cities — big enough for a local-first agency, small enough that chamber + referral presence can cover it.
2. **Best-fit verticals:** healthcare-adjacent SMBs (private medical/dental/PT practices riding the Kaiser/Sutter/Adventist expansion), home services (fed by high-income homeowners), auto dealerships (Automall, 21 brands), retail, and professional services — all named leading industries in both cities.
3. **Lead with the missed-call/no-web-presence pain**: local data (62% missed calls, 90% no organic presence) maps directly to AI receptionist, chatbot, and follow-up automation offers.
4. **Positioning gap:** no established Rocklin/Roseville-branded AI automation agency exists; competitors are Sacramento-branded, national, or SEO-first (PipelineOS). A hyper-local "South Placer AI automation" identity is available.
5. **Pricing:** anchor SMB retainers at $500–$2,000/month with $3K–$10K setup projects, consistent with national norms; local incomes support the upper half of those ranges.
6. **Channel priority:** join both chambers (Roseville breakfasts = 100+ owners per event; Rocklin = 600+ members), get into a BNI Capital Region chapter, and offer free "Intro to AI" talks via SBDC/chamber programming — the SBDC is already priming demand with AI-education events, and Build Up Rocklin is a direct line to scale-ready local businesses.
